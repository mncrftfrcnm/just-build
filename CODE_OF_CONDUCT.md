# Community conduct

Keep collaboration respectful, specific and focused on the project. Welcome questions and differing experience levels. Critique behavior and code with reproducible evidence; avoid personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, threats, spam or publishing another person's private information.

Do not post credentials, private chat histories or other sensitive data in issues, reviews or examples. Use fictional reproductions and the [security reporting process](SECURITY.md) when appropriate.

Maintainers may edit/remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions or restrict participation using the repository platform's moderation tools. For a conduct concern, use a private platform reporting option when available; otherwise request a private contact without publishing sensitive details.
